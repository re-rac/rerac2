# Changelog

## v0.1.6-alpha

Every planet of the galaxy now has its creatures, machines and people.

- Barlow, Notak, Siberius, Tabora, Dobbo, Joba, Todano, Boldan, the Aranos prison, Snivelak, Smolg, Damosel, Grelbin
  and Yeedil: their enemies, bosses, lifts, traps, puzzles and the people you talk to.
- The space fights of Feltzin, Hrugis and Gorn, the Giant Clank fights in orbit, the hoverbike races and the
  Insomniac Museum.
- The hovership and the glider rides, Clank's commands to the robots, and the crystal and raritanium collecting.
- The energy barriers and force fields are drawn, and water behaves the same on every planet.
- Crates break into pieces everywhere, enemies flinch under a stream of fire, and the parked ship rocks as you stand on
  it.
- The Heavy Lancer fires its own shots, the Lock On mod works, the pad rumbles, and the game saves on its own.

## v0.1.5-alpha

Maktar and Endako can now be played through, and most of the arsenal is in Ratchet's hands.

- Maktar: the arena with its challenges, crowd, announcer, bosses and prizes, the slot machines, the power nodes and
  the bomb catapult, and the Jamming Array.
- Endako: the remote crane, the sentries and flame turrets, the arena locks, playing as Clank, and the boss fight.
- Oozla's Swamp Monster II and its arena.
- The Swingshot, the Spiderbot Glove, the Chopper, the Electrolyzer and the Hacker's puzzles.
- Nearly every weapon from the vendors, with their upgrades, mods and experience bar, and Clank's packs.
- First person, gravity ramps, the Specials menu with Skill Points and Cheats.
- Shadows under characters, dust at Ratchet's feet, footprints in the snow, and sliding on ice.
- The first creatures and machines of Barlow, Notak, Siberius, Joba, Todano and Boldan.

## v0.1.2-alpha

Ratchet can now leave Aranos. The flight to Oozla plays out with its story scenes, and Oozla, the Wupash Nebula space
fight and the first steps on Maktar follow.

- Oozla: its swamp water, lily pads, swamp creatures, the snapping flowers, the Dynamo and its platforms, the Tractor
  Beam and the boxes it drags, the teleporters and the swamp beast you ride.
- The Wupash Nebula: the ship's flight, lasers, missiles and boost, the fighters, gunships and asteroids, and the
  ship's HUD.
- The vendor: its hologram, the weapon preview and buying.
- The Gravity Bomb, and the quick select ring for switching weapons.
- Bolts shine, tumble and fly to Ratchet as in the original.
- Nanotech crates heal Ratchet, ammo crates throw ammo, and crate stacks fall when one below breaks.
- Fog, flight sounds, the ship's exhaust, and many fixes to enemies, lifts and doors.

## v0.1.1-alpha

The first version of ReRAC2. The game boots to its main menu, and New Game starts on Aranos, the first planet.

- Aranos's ship is lit as in the original: its walls, floors, props and lights.
- Enemies flash red and hop back when you hit them, and blow apart when they die.
- The nanotech meter lines up and pulses as in the original.
- Fullscreen and the render resolution can be changed while you play.
